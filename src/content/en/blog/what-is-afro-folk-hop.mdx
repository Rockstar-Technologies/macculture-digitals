Afro Folk Hop is a music genre that blends elements of African folk music, hip-hop and other contemporary styles. The term was coined and popularized by Mac Kelly, and materialized by the release of an album and an instrumental follow-up under that name in 2022.

## A fusion of old and new

It mixes traditional African sounds, such as storytelling, vocals and instruments like drums and strings, with modern hip-hop production and beats.

## A vehicle for culture

It tells relatable, culture-rooted stories to new generations while keeping heritage sounds alive.
